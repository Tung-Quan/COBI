# Scope and limitations

- **Population and chronology.** UCI is a Taiwan credit-card dataset from 2005. The stratified holdout has disjoint sample IDs. It cannot establish out-of-time, cross-product, or country transfer validity.
- **Repeated patterns.** The published dataset contains 817 duplicate predictor rows beyond the first occurrence of a vector. In the locked test, 127 rows have predictor vectors also observed in training. Unique sample IDs do not identify customer groups or distinguish repeated observations from coincident vectors. The row-bootstrap intervals assume independence, which may understate uncertainty under unknown grouping. A new study would need independently reserved rows or a source-supported grouped cohort.
- **Calibration and fairness.** The test slope and intercept depart from ideal calibration within the reported bootstrap model. Tail bins have few records. Demographic exclusion is a predictor restriction. Proxy effects, subgroup performance, calibration stability and fairness remain unvalidated.
- **P2–P4.** Evidence counts and verification flags are inputs to synthetic fixtures. No document extractor, real factual verification, or defect-labeled study has been executed. CS remains `UNVALIDATED_EVIDENCE_SCORE`.
- **P5–P7.** DTI cap, residual buffer, synthetic coefficients and LGD are illustrative assumptions. Principal is the scenario EAD basis. A real expected loss estimate needs compatible calibrated risk, horizon and recoveries.
- **P8–P10.** E5 compares assumed route costs on six cases with a fixed synthetic comparator. The reported differences are arithmetic, without observed counterfactuals, time, conversion, causal savings or other bank outcomes. The original presentation KPI figures are targets.
- **External data.** `BANK_EXTERNAL_VALIDATION_BLOCKED`. Home Credit requires Kaggle rule acceptance and authorized access. Give Me Some Credit and CORD were optional and were not acquired. Lawful partner access, label maturity, customer grouping, cohort drift, independent reviews, and a controlled pilot remain future work.
- **Application boundary.** This is a local research/demo workspace. SQLite triggers protect against application-level edits and deletes of audit events. Production authentication, KYC, secured bank integration, real consent management, access control, operational monitoring and data retention are outside this release. The research queue is served by a local operator running the fixed command. An interrupted process requires checking its log and test marker.
