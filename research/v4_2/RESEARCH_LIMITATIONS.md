# Research Limitations

- The available benchmark is synthetic. It does not establish bank performance, validity, deployment safety, credit quality, fraud attribution or savings.
- Generator C is unblinded and exploratory. Its historical metrics are not confirmatory V4.2 evidence.
- D1.1 has an internally reproduced label/rule disagreement; proposed corrections are unadjudicated and cannot be treated as gold.
- No independent real financial evidence set, approved target population, data permission, reviewer panel or bank risk tolerance is present.
- The scoped systematic search and first-pass screen are documented in `SYSTEMATIC_SEARCH_PROTOCOL.md` and the 28-entry screening log (26 unique studies/resources plus two explicit version/duplicate views). Retrieval counts are not exportable from the search interface and there was no second screener; novelty is therefore unestablished. Some data/source licenses remain unverified.
- FinDVer paper and ACL abstract disagree on dataset size/subset count; both claims are preserved pending authoritative resolution.
- Two historic manifest discrepancies remain unresolved; checksum failure is an integrity blocker, not an invitation to replace historical manifests.
- Draft schemas specify shape, not truth, authority, privacy compliance, reviewer expertise, or source-span correctness.
- Proposed uncertainty/risk methods depend on sampling and exchangeability assumptions that may fail under source, time, policy or institution shift.
- Cost coefficients and workforce savings are assumptions until measured with approved operational data; no cost reduction is claimed.
