# Research Gap and Bounded Novelty Claims

## What the reviewed literature already covers

Financial numerical QA (FinQA, TAT-QA), financial claim verification (FinDVer), financial document error detection (FinED-Bench), multi-document accounting reconciliation (FinBalance), distribution-shift benchmarks (WILDS), data provenance, temporal data models, selective classification and conformal risk control are all established lines of work. A broad claim such as “first financial evidence verifier,” “first multi-document financial verifier,” or “first risk-aware review system” is not supported and is rejected here.

The closest recent comparison is **FinBalance**, which already has multi-document accounting source bundles, policy-controlled reconciliation, cited outputs and inconsistency labels; it uses a deterministic ledger benchmark and reports expert validation. **FinED-Bench** directly addresses document-level financial error detection using real source documents with injected errors. **FinDVer** addresses explainable claim verification in long hybrid financial documents. Its authoritative records disagree: ACL published abstract says 4,000/four subsets, arXiv abstract says 2,400/three, and ACL PDF body says 2,100 initial examples across three (600 testmini + 1,500 test); the author README describes three subsets and a July 2026 test-label release but no final total. No source establishes why. These papers narrow a plausible COBI contribution to a task-specific benchmark and carefully separated endpoints, if those are demonstrated with independent data and adjudication.

## Candidate contribution (hypothesis, not novelty established)

The proposed work could study **borrower/case-level evidence review** where each pair has a tri-state comparability label, a separate relation label, directional amendment/supersession, provenance and valid/recorded time, and a separate human reference action. The proposed evaluation would distinguish (i) conflict detection, (ii) document attribution supported by independent evidence, and (iii) case review workload and critical misses under preapproved risk constraints, across generator/source/time/policy shifts.

These elements may be an integration of existing methods rather than a new method. The claim should be retained only if a full-text comparison with FinBalance, FinED-Bench, FinDVer and broader audit/reconciliation systems shows a materially different and useful task/evaluation design, and an independent benchmark demonstrates it.

## Novelty decision

**Current disposition: `NOVELTY_UNESTABLISHED`.** The Phase 1 work supplies an ontology, benchmark contract and falsifiable questions. It does not establish a new algorithm or benchmark contribution, and it reports no V4.2 performance result. The closest full texts were inspected, but systematic coverage, data/license review, broader task overlap analysis and independent labeled data remain prerequisites.


## Final Phase 1 literature-screen update (2026-11-11)

The scoped search and first-pass screen now include FinFIRST (expert-authored source-backed research tasks), HC-RAG/Multi-Doc-2025 (cross-document evidence graph and evidence localization), EvidenceLens (claim-evidence matrix and review prioritization), and FinAgent Benchmark v1.1.1 (row-level gold correction history). These close additional gaps in the earlier review. They further rule out novelty claims based on evidence graphs, source-backed financial answers, claim-evidence matrices, review prioritization, or annotation-audit mechanics alone.

The remaining candidate distinction is a **proposed task decomposition**: pair comparability/relation, independently verified document attribution with unresolved status, and borrower/case-level selective actions with explicit denominators and risk constraints. No primary source reviewed establishes that exact conjunction for an independently labeled borrower-case population; that absence in a scoped, single-reviewer search is not evidence of novelty. Final status remains `NOVELTY_UNESTABLISHED`. The screening log has 28 entries (26 unique studies/resources plus two explicit version/duplicate views); no stable total retrieval count or second screener is available.
